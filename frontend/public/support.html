Ryoko

Support

Ryoko is currently a PWA-first beta. The final support email is not set yet.

Reporting Import Issues

When reporting PDF or barcode problems, include your device, browser, airline, and a redacted ticket sample. Keep the flight number, route, date, and layout visible, but hide passport numbers, full ticket numbers, payment details, loyalty numbers, and any sensitive identifiers.

Data Requests

Use the in-app export and delete tools for local data. For synced accounts, backend deletion will be handled from the app once Supabase sync is configured.

Beta Contact

Until a production support address is published, use the private beta channel where you received access to Ryoko.
