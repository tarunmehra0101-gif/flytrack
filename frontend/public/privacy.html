Ryoko

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 May 2026

Overview

Ryoko is a personal travel ledger for frequent flyers. It helps you import boarding passes and tickets, save flight history, calculate travel analytics, and view routes on a map. This policy is written for an India-based startup building a privacy-first public beta.

Information We Process

Ryoko may process your name, email address, profile preferences, home airport, flight details, ticket metadata, imported boarding pass text, analytics snapshots, and map-related travel summaries. Ticket PDFs and barcode images are parsed locally where possible. Confirmed flight records may sync to your account if you enable cloud login.

Local-First Ticket Handling

For the PWA beta, Ryoko is designed to parse tickets on your device whenever possible. Raw ticket files are not uploaded by default. The app stores confirmed travel records and user-approved fields, not a permanent archive of private ticket PDFs, unless a future version asks for explicit permission.

Account Sync

If you sign in with Google through Supabase, Ryoko syncs your confirmed ledger so you can access it from another browser or device. Access is protected by authentication and row-level security so users can only access their own records.

Third-Party Services

Ryoko may use Supabase for authentication and database storage, Google for sign-in, open airport datasets such as OurAirports, and optional flight-status providers such as Aviationstack. API keys are kept server-side where required.

How We Use Data

We use your data to provide the app experience: flight import, timeline, analytics, city and airport summaries, map routes, yearly Wrapped cards, export, backup, and account sync. We do not sell your personal travel data.

Deletion And Export

You can export your local ledger and delete travel data from the app. For synced accounts, deletion should remove your records from the backend as well. During beta, contact details may be updated once Ryoko has a formal support channel.

Children

Ryoko is not intended for children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact the operator so it can be removed.

Contact

Support contact is pending. Until a final support email/domain is published, beta testers should report issues through the channel where they received access to Ryoko.
